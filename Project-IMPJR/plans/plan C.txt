Model architectures
Use a modular ensemble: each model specializes in a core market dimension (direction, volatility, liquidity, regime). Combine outputs with stacking and probabilistic calibration.
Core ensemble
- Directional sequence model (1h–24h):
Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT) / Transformer-LSTM hybrid with multi-horizon outputs (mean and quantiles). Inputs include klines (spot/futures/mark/index), premiums/funding, basis, OI/long-short ratios, BVOL, and option EOHSummary features. Designed to capture seasonality, exogenous drivers, and attention-based interpretability.
- Volatility model (intra-day to next 24h):
Realized volatility + EGARCH/HAR-RV + volatility Transformer, generating probabilistic forecasts of realized vol, drawdown risk, and high–low ranges. Incorporates BVOL index, option implied metrics, and realized microstructure features.
- Microstructure model (seconds–minutes):
Order book Transformer/TCN + Hawkes process head to model trade arrivals, imbalance, short-term drift, and price impact. Feeds features upstream to nowcasting components of the directional model.
- Regime classifier:
Markov Switching/HMM + clustering (k-means/DBSCAN) + shallow Transformer to tag regimes (trend, mean-reversion, high-vol/liquidity crunch). Regime tags condition the ensemble (e.g., dynamic weights, horizon-specific risk).
- Event risk classifier:
Gradient-boosted trees / shallow Transformer to predict tail events (≥X% move, liquidation clusters, basis dislocations). Outputs probabilities and expected severity for risk management.
- Basis and funding arbitrage module:
Sequence regression (LightGBM/XGBoost + Transformer features) to forecast futures-spot basis, funding rate shifts, and premium index changes; used as exogenous inputs to directional/vol models.
Fusion and calibration
- Stacking/meta-learner:
Regularized logistic/linear meta-model combines directional, volatility, and event signals into final probabilistic forecasts for return, high-low, and tail-risk.
- Probabilistic calibration:
Isotonic regression / Platt scaling / quantile recalibration to align predicted probabilities and quantiles with empirical frequencies across regimes.

Feature engineering
Engineer features at three layers: microstructure, market structure, and derivatives-informed risk. Ensure every feature is timestamp-aligned and leakage-safe.
Microstructure and order book
- Trade intensity:
Rate, inter-arrival times, buy/sell imbalance, aggressive vs passive flow, and signed volume from aggTrades/trades.
- Order book signals:
Best bid/ask, mid, spreads, top-level and multi-level depth, imbalance, depth elasticity, and slippage proxies from bookTicker/bookDepth.
- Price impact metrics:
Short-horizon impact of net order flow, Kyle’s lambda estimates, cost of crossing spread, and microprice deviation.
- Market state flags:
Liquidity drought indicators, quote stuffing patterns, and rapid depth withdrawals.
Price and returns
- Returns spectrum:
Log returns across multiple windows (1m–4h), momentum, mean-reversion strength, and shock flags.
- High–low structure:
True range, realized volatility (RV), bipower variation, realized skew/kurtosis.
- Cross-venue normalization:
Spot vs futures spreads; mark vs index deviations; basis roll-down.
Derivatives and funding
- Futures basis and premium:
Premium index klines; spot–mark/index differences; basis slope and convexity over horizons.
- Funding rates:
Latest and lagged funding; anticipated funding from basis/premium; funding seasonality and regime tags.
- Open interest and positioning:
Sum OI, OI changes, top-trader long-short ratios, taker long/short vol ratio; inferred positioning pressure.
- Liquidations:
Liquidation snapshot intensity, side asymmetry, price distance to mass liquidation bands.
Options and implied risk
- BVOL index:
Absolute level, changes, and term structure proxies via dynamics; realized vs implied vol spread.
- EOHSummary greeks:
Delta/gamma/vega/theta aggregates by moneyness and tenor; skew/smile slope; IV term structure; mark IV momentum.
- Gamma exposure proxies:
Net gamma regime indicators (estimated via aggregated contract data), expected dealer positioning pressure.
Cross-market and macro signals (optional if available)
- Risk proxies:
Stablecoin flows, ETF flows, funding dispersion across venues, if accessible.
- Time-of-day/seasonality:
UTC hour dummies, weekday/weekend effects, expiry/roll dates.
Target definitions
- Directional targets:
Next-24h return, and multi-horizon returns (1h/4h/12h/24h).
- Range targets:
Next-24h high–low band, quantile forecasts (q_{10},q_{50},q_{90}).
- Risk targets:
Tail-event probabilities (≥X% move), drawdown exceedance, liquidation cluster probability.

Training datasets and labeling
Build supervised learning datasets per module, ensuring historical coverage and regime diversity.
Data assembly
- Unified timeline:
Merge spot/futures/options feeds on a canonical 1m grid; compute microstructure features on 1s/5s and aggregate with leakage-safe windows.
- Feature lags:
Create lag stacks (e.g., 1–240 minutes) and rolling statistics; prevent future leakage by truncating at prediction time.
- Cross-sectional augmentation:
Include USDⓈ-M and COIN-M variants; align spot and mark/index; derive cross-instrument spreads and differentials.
Labeling and horizons
- Directional labels:
Forward returns over 1h, 4h, 12h, 24h; classify sign for classification heads; maintain continuous labels for regression.
- Volatility labels:
Realized vol from intraday high–low/RV; drawdown/maximum excursion; quantiles via empirical order statistics.
- Event labels:
Binary flags for large moves, basis dislocations, liquidation bursts; thresholds set per regime to avoid class imbalance extremes.
Sampling and splits
- Regime-aware splits:
Time-based splits preserving chronology; stratify by regimes (vol/momentum/liquidity) to test generalization.
- Cross-validation:
Rolling-window CV; blocked folds to avoid leakage; nested CV for hyperparameters with limited retraining frequency.
- Class imbalance handling:
Focal loss or class weights for event models; balanced subsampling with cost-sensitive metrics.

Real-time system and precision strategies
Precision isn’t just modeling—it’s the full system: live ingestion, adaptive ensembling, calibration, and risk-aware post-processing.
Ingestion and feature computation
- Websocket integration:
Stream bookTicker/bookDepth/trades/aggTrades and compute microstructure features in-memory with low-latency queues.
- Hierarchical features:
Maintain fast-path (order book + trade intensity) and slow-path (funding, OI, BVOL, EOHSummary) with appropriate refresh cadence.
- Drift detection:
Monitor feature distributions (PSI/KS tests) and performance residuals; trigger recalibration or fallback weights.
Adaptive ensemble and nowcasting
- Regime-conditioned weights:
Adjust model weights based on regime classifier outputs; emphasize microstructure model during high-liquidity/trend regimes, volatility model during stressed regimes.
- Short-term nowcasts:
Use microstructure outputs to update intraday risk and direction, feeding into the 24h forecast as a dynamic prior.
- Uncertainty quantification:
Produce prediction intervals and tail probabilities; prefer decision rules based on calibrated risk-adjusted signals rather than point estimates.
