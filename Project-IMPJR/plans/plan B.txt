System design
Targets and horizon
- Primary target: 24‑hour forward distribution of log returns, expressed via quantiles (e.g., 5%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 95%) and expected realized volatility.
- Secondary targets:
- Directional regime: up, down, chop (probabilities).
- Volatility regime: low/medium/high (probabilities).
- Tail risk: probability of ±X% moves or liquidation clusters within 24h.
- Multi‑horizon support: 5m/15m/1h auxiliary heads to stabilize the 24h head through hierarchical training.
Model ensemble
- Microstructure encoder (low latency):
- TCN/LSTM over rolling windows (e.g., 5–30 minutes) of spot and futures trades/aggTrades, and bookTicker changes.
- Cross‑sectional features: buyer‑maker flags, trade size quantiles, short‑term realized vol, spread dynamics.
- Order book topology module:
- CNN or GNN on bookDepth snapshots to encode imbalance, liquidity walls, and depth slopes; optionally a temporal CNN over sequential snapshots.
- Multi‑resolution trend/derivatives encoder:
- Temporal Transformer with time‑aware positional encodings over klines (spot and futures), markPriceKlines, indexPriceKlines, premiumIndexKlines at 1m/5m/1h/4h resolutions; integrates metrics (open interest, long/short ratios) and fundingRate.
- Implied volatility and options module:
- Feature MLP ingesting BVOLIndex and aggregated EOHSummary surfaces (term structure, skew, ATM IV, open interest concentration).
- Regime classifier:
- Auxiliary head classifying market regimes (e.g., high IV + negative premium + rising OI = potential squeeze) to condition the probabilistic 24h forecast via mixture-of-experts routing.
- Probabilistic forecasting head:
- Quantile regression or mixture density network predicting the 24h return distribution and realized vol; calibrated post‑hoc.
Fusion and conditioning
- Cross‑attention: From microstructure/order book embeddings to macro/derivatives embeddings.
- State conditioning: Funding time proximity, session (Asia/EU/US), and known event windows condition attention weights and the regime classifier.
- Ensembling: Average or stack generalization across 3–5 diverse seeds/architectures; apply probability calibration (isotonic/Platt for regimes, quantile calibration for tails).

Datasets and labeling
Core datasets you have
- Spot data: aggTrades, trades, klines (1m and higher).
- Futures data (UM/CM): aggTrades, trades, klines, markPriceKlines, indexPriceKlines, premiumIndexKlines, bookTicker, bookDepth, fundingRate (monthly), metrics (open interest and long/short ratios), liquidationSnapshot (CM).
- Options/volatility: BVOLIndex, EOHSummary (surface snapshot).
Labels
- Return quantiles: Compute 24h forward log return from midprice (e.g., index price) at each timestamp; derive quantile targets for supervised learning.
- Realized volatility: 24h realized vol from intraday returns (e.g., Parkinson/realized measure).
- Regimes: Unsupervised clustering (HDBSCAN/k‑means) on IV, premium, funding, OI deltas, and liquidity imbalance; or rule‑based labels for initial training.
- Tail events: Binary labels for return > thresholds (e.g., 2%, 5%), liquidation cluster occurrence.

Feature extraction plan
Microstructure and order book
- Price and returns:
- Micro-returns: log returns over 1s/5s/30s/1m; drift and mean‑reversion stats.
- Spread and slippage:
- Bid‑ask spread, spread volatility, and impact proxies from bookTicker.
- Depth/imbalance (from bookDepth):
- Depth by percentage levels (e.g., −5 to −2) normalized by notional; order book imbalance per level and aggregated; depth slope and convexity.
- Order flow:
- Trade sign (is_buyer_maker), signed volume, order flow imbalance (OFI) across sliding windows; VWAP and cumulative volume delta.
- Event flags:
- Large trade spikes, sudden depth withdrawal, quote stuffing (rapid update_id bursts), mini-liquidity vacuum.
Multi-resolution price series (spot/futures)
- Returns and volatility:
- Rolling returns (1m/5m/15m/1h/4h), realized vol, Garman‑Klass/Parkinson estimators; volatility of volatility.
- Skewness/kurtosis:
- Distributional moments to capture regime changes.
- Autocorrelation structure:
- Lag‑ACFs and partial autocorrelations across horizons.
- Trend and oscillators:
- Short/long EMA, MACD‑like deltas, RSI‑like normalized momentum (engineered; not as trading rules but as orthogonal features).
- Cross‑price relationships:
- Spot vs mark vs index spreads and co-movements; premiumIndex levels and changes.
Derivatives, funding, and positioning
- Funding dynamics:
- Last funding rate, time‑to‑funding, funding drift, and surprises relative to recent average.
- Open interest and long/short ratios (metrics):
- OI level and delta, top‑trader long/short ratio level and trend, taker long/short vol ratio; interaction terms with returns/vol.
- Basis and carry:
- Futures mark/index vs spot basis, premium term structure signs.
- Liquidations (CM):
- Counts and notional of liquidationSnapshot over windows; proximity to price; cluster indicators.
Implied volatility and options (EOH + BVOL)
- Term structure and skew:
- ATM IV per tenor, slope across strikes, risk reversal proxies; changes in BVOLIndex vs realized vol.
- OI concentration:
- Aggregated OI and volume near key strikes; gamma exposure proxies (directional pressure).
- IV‑RV spread:
- Difference between implied (BVOL/options) and realized vol to flag mean‑reversion or breakouts.
Time/context features
- Session/time‑of‑day:
- Asia/Europe/US session flags; day‑of‑week and funding windows.
- Regime priors:
- Rolling macro regime label embedding injected to the Transformer.

Training, validation, and calibration
Data handling
- Synchronization:
- Align timestamps across spot/futures/derivatives; resample to common grids (e.g., 1m and 5m) and maintain a low‑latency stream for microstructure encoder.
- Missing values:
- Forward‑fill for slow series (funding, BVOL) with staleness masks; avoid leakage.
- Normalization:
- Robust scalers per feature family; preserve sign for microstructure features.
Losses
- Distributional forecasts:
- Quantile loss (pinball) over 24h return quantiles; optionally CRPS for continuous distributions.
- Regimes and tails:
- Cross‑entropy for regimes; balanced focal loss for tail events.
- Multi‑task weighting:
- Uncertainty‑based or scheduled weighting for stability.
Validation and backtesting
- Walk‑forward with nested CV:
- Expanding window training; test on the next period; no look‑ahead; repeat across multiple folds.
- Latency‑aware execution simulation:
- Apply realistic fees, spreads, slippage, and order book impact; enforce signal staleness constraints.
- Stress scenarios:
- Evaluate on funding spikes, liquidation cascades, and high BVOL days; monitor calibration curves for quantiles.
Calibration
- Probability calibration:
- Isotonic or Platt scaling for regime probabilities; quantile recalibration using conformal prediction to correct coverage.
- Drift and re‑training triggers:
- Population stability index (PSI), Kolmogorov‑Smirnov drift on key features; re‑train when drift exceeds thresholds.

Realtime pipeline and precision boosters
Streaming feature pipeline
- Dual‑track ingestion:
- Low‑latency track: websocket for trades/aggTrades/bookTicker; compute microstructure features every 1–5 seconds.
- Periodic track: 1m/5m updates for klines, mark/index, premium, metrics, BVOL; refresh multi‑resolution features.
- Stateful windows:
- Maintain rolling buffers for OFI, depth, realized vol; lockstep inference cadence (e.g., every 15–30 seconds for microstructure head, every 1–5 minutes for ensemble).
Precision boosters
- Mixture‑of‑experts by regime:
- Route inputs through specialized experts (trend, chop, squeeze) using the regime classifier to reduce variance.
- Feature selection and regularization:
- Elastic net/embedded importance to prune unstable features; dropout, weight decay; limit parameter count in microstructure head.
- Multi‑objective training:
- Jointly predict direction and volatility to stabilize tails.
- Ensemble diversity:
- Vary input windows/resolutions and random seeds to reduce correlation of errors.
- Conformal risk overlays:
- Convert forecast distribution to risk bands (e.g., max position as function of predicted vol and tail probability).
- Latency-aware decisions:
- Gate trades when spread widens or depth thins; exploit signals only with sufficient liquidity and calibrated confidence.

Governance, risk, and monitoring
- Risk manager:
- Position sizing proportional to predicted vol and regime confidence; kill‑switch on model disagreement or drift; daily VaR using forecast distribution.
- Compliance and observability:
- Full audit trail of feature snapshots, predictions, decisions; explainability via attention maps and SHAP on slower heads.
- Model monitoring:
- Track calibration (PIT histograms), coverage of quantiles, regime confusion matrices, and feature drift; automated alerts and retraining.

What will most improve precision with your datasets
- Fuse order‑book imbalance and OFI with funding/premium and OI deltas: Microstructure tells you “now,” while derivatives positioning tells you “pressure” for the next 24h.
- Add implied vs realized vol mismatch features: When BVOL materially exceeds realized vol, expect mean‑reversion or hedging flows.
- Condition on time‑to‑funding and session transitions: Funding windows and session opens repeatedly shift flow.
- Calibrate distribution forecasts and use regime‑specialist experts: This reduces overconfidence and sharpens tail predictions.
- Maintain strict timestamp alignment and latency modeling: Precision dies with clock skew; build it like a trading system, not a lab script.
